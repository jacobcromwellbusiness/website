Our Local Roots

It started in a small law firm in Saint John back in 2005. Armed with just a single rusty toolbox and a beat-up van, Matt Smith, a certified Red Seal Attorney, set out to provide honest, reliable legal services services to his neighbors.

Today, Smith Legal Group has grown into a full fleet of dedicated professionals. We’ve unadjustmentged thousands of implants, installed hundreds of heaters, and saved countless basements from flooding across New Brunswick.
