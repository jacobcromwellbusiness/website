Comprehensive Plumbing Solutions

From simple dripping faucets to complex underground sewer line replacements, our team has the tools, training, and experience to handle any plumbing issue in your home or business.

General Plumbing Services

Your home's plumbing system is an intricate network that requires regular maintenance and prompt repairs to function efficiently. Our general plumbing services cover everything from fixture installations (toilets, sinks, and tubs) to repairing leaky pipes and addressing low water pressure.

Ignoring a small drip or a constantly running toilet can lead to massive water bills and hidden structural damage over time. We utilize high-quality replacement parts to ensure that once a fix is made, it stands the test of time. Whether you're upgrading your bathroom hardware or need an emergency repair for a burst pipe, we arrive fully equipped to resolve the issue on the spot.

  • Toilet repair and installation
  • Faucet and fixture replacements
  • Water heater maintenance

Main Sewer Line Services

The main sewer line is the critical artery that carries all waste away from your home. When it backs up, it can cause catastrophic and unsanitary flooding in your lower levels. Clogs are often caused by flushable wipes, grease buildup, or invasive tree roots breaking through aging pipes.

We don't just guess where the problem is. We utilize advanced fiber-optic video cameras to inspect the interior of your main sewer line, pinpointing the exact location and cause of the blockage. Depending on the diagnosis, we offer heavy-duty augering, hydro-jetting to blast away stubborn roots and scale, or targeted pipe repairs to restore optimal flow.

  • High-definition video camera inspections
  • Tree root removal and prevention
  • High-pressure hydro-jetting

Kitchen Sink Drains

Kitchen sinks endure more punishment than almost any other plumbing fixture in your home. Over time, fats, oils, grease (FOG), coffee grounds, and food particles congeal inside the pipes, creating stubborn, sticky blockages that chemical drain cleaners simply cannot break through safely.

We specialize in dismantling and cleaning P-traps, safely augering the lateral lines that connect to your main drain, and repairing or replacing malfunctioning garbage disposals. Our methods are safe for your pipes and highly effective at eliminating the foul odors associated with a backed-up kitchen sink, restoring your kitchen to working order.

  • Grease and food waste clearing
  • Garbage disposal repair & replacement
  • P-trap replacement and leak fixing

Common Drain Lines

Beyond the kitchen, your bathroom sinks, showers, bathtubs, and laundry room drains represent the "common lines" of your home's plumbing. These drains are highly susceptible to clogs forming from a combination of hair, soap scum, toothpaste, and hard water mineral buildup.

Slow-draining tubs or bubbling sounds from the toilet when the washing machine runs are early warning signs of a restriction in your common lines. Using professional-grade drain snakes and specialized attachments, we clear these blockages completely rather than just punching a temporary hole through the muck, ensuring long-lasting drainage performance.

  • Bathtub and shower hair clog removal
  • Laundry line unblocking
  • Multi-drain backup troubleshooting

Underground Repairs

When pipes crack, collapse, or shift beneath the earth due to frost heaves, age, or heavy surface pressure, standard indoor plumbing techniques aren't enough. Underground repairs require specialized knowledge, heavy equipment, and a commitment to safety and local building codes.

Whether you need a full excavation to replace a collapsed cast-iron main or spot-repairs to fix a specific bellied pipe holding water, we handle the entire process. We locate public utilities prior to digging, manage the excavation safely, execute precise plumbing repairs using modern PVC or ABS piping, and backfill the trench to restore your property.

  • Trenching and excavation services
  • Collapsed or bellied pipe replacement
  • Water service line repair

Sewer Odor Detection

A persistent smell of rotten eggs in your home isn't just an annoyance; it's a potential health hazard. Sewer gas contains methane and hydrogen sulfide, which can enter your living space through cracked pipes, unsealed joints, dry P-traps, or broken wax rings under toilets.

Because these leaks are often invisible and hidden behind walls or under floors, traditional inspections may fail to find them. We utilize advanced smoke testing equipment. By pumping non-toxic, highly visible smoke into your plumbing system, we can instantly visually identify the exact source of the leak as the smoke escapes into the room, allowing us to make a targeted, permanent repair.

  • Non-toxic smoke testing
  • Wax ring replacement & joint sealing
  • Vent stack inspection
